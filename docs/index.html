Ignite 2026 / Stage 2

Drag a team from the pool into the list to place it. Drag rows to reorder; the numbers renumber as you go.

EMEA, Americas and Global are three separate rankings. Rank the two regions first, then hit Seed from EU + NA in Global to start from them rather than an empty list.

Click a team to open its roster here, then click any name to change it. Rate players S to F and each team shows the placement its roster implies.

The six dots on each team are its starters: solid confirmed, hollow an open slot, amber a leak.

Hide roster gives the ranking the full width, or drag the divider to size the panel however you like.
